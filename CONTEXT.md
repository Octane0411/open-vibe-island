# Orbit Domain Glossary

## Orbit

The GPLv3 macOS application being built from the Open Island implementation base. Orbit is the only runtime, notch/menu-bar owner, activity surface, and settings owner in the finished product.

## Vibe Island parity

Observable parity with Vibe Island in physical-notch and menu-bar placement, expansion and collapse motion, information density, interaction timing, settings depth, and overall polish. It does not require Vibe Island branding, assets, proprietary implementation, or identical backend behavior.

## Orbit identity

Orbit's own name, visual language, symbols, copy, settings namespace, and product positioning. Orbit identity remains distinct even when Vibe Island parity is the presentation acceptance standard.

## Implementation base

The last published GPLv3 Open Island source and preserved Git history from which Orbit is forked.

## Capability source

A licensed external project whose compatible components or patterns may be incorporated with required attribution. CodeIsland, headroom-desktop, and Headroom are capability sources under the boundaries in `docs/orbit/reference-intake.md`.

## Behavior reference

A product whose publicly visible behavior may define requirements but whose implementation, assets, branding, and protected UI are not copied. Vibe Island and AgentPeek are behavior references.

## Compact default

Orbit starts collapsed and remains a small notch/menu-bar status surface during ordinary running, thinking, usage, and idle changes.

## Automatic expansion policy

Only approval and question prompts auto-expand into a full actionable surface. Completion uses a small temporary card. Hover and click can expand the session surface, and each automatic behavior is user-configurable in Settings.
